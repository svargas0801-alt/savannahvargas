Restaurant Group

Angelina's Kitchen & Ristorante

Developed a multi-location marketing strategy that increased audience growth by 988% while strengthening brand identity across multiple restaurant concepts.

+988% Audience Growth 2.4M Impressions 42,506 Engagements 424K Video Views

Challenge

Develop a cohesive marketing strategy across multiple restaurant concepts while increasing brand awareness, engagement, and customer loyalty.

Business Goals

  • Increase awareness across all restaurant locations
  • Strengthen a consistent brand identity across multiple concepts
  • Grow qualified foot traffic, reservations, and event inquiries
  • Build long-term customer loyalty and repeat visits

Strategy

  • Brand architecture for multiple locations
  • Story-driven hospitality marketing
  • Community engagement and customer interaction
  • Event and wedding promotion
  • Consistent brand messaging across locations
  • Organic content focused on food, experiences, and culture

Execution

  • Produced on-site photo and video shoots at each location
  • Built location-specific content calendars under one shared brand system
  • Ran organic campaigns spotlighting menu items, chef stories, and culture
  • Coordinated wedding and private event promotion with the sales team

Lessons Learned

Consistency across multiple locations requires a shared content system, not one-off posts. Building repeatable templates let the brand scale without losing its voice — and story-driven content built around people, not just plates, consistently outperformed straight promotional posts.

My Role

I led strategy, account management, and creative direction across all Angelina's locations — coordinating photographers and videographers, managing content calendars, and reporting performance directly to ownership.

Flash Security Integrators

Humanized a security brand through storytelling, influencer partnerships, and localized marketing campaigns.

416K Impressions 264K Video Views 20,000%+ Engagement Growth

Challenge

Increase brand awareness, create stronger emotional connections with customers, and position Flash Security as more than a security provider in a competitive market.

Business Goals

  • Differentiate Flash from competing security providers
  • Build emotional trust with residential and commercial customers
  • Generate qualified leads across multiple service areas
  • Humanize a traditionally technical, impersonal industry

Strategy

  • Humanize the brand through employee and culture-focused content
  • Influencer partnerships and brand collaborations
  • Geo-targeted advertising campaigns
  • Localized marketing across multiple service areas
  • Community engagement and customer interaction
  • Continuous testing and performance optimization

Execution

  • Built an employee- and culture-focused content series
  • Secured influencer and brand partnerships to extend reach
  • Launched geo-targeted paid campaigns by service area
  • Continuously tested creative and messaging to see what drove link clicks

Luxury Hospitality

Oyster Point & Molly Pitcher Hotel

Positioned a luxury waterfront destination through hospitality-focused content, tourism marketing, and premium brand storytelling.

Luxury Hospitality Marketing Content Strategy Brand Storytelling

Challenge

Increase visibility for two luxury waterfront hospitality properties while driving awareness for weddings, events, dining experiences, overnight stays, and seasonal promotions.

Business Goals

  • Increase visibility for two waterfront luxury properties
  • Drive awareness and bookings for weddings, events, and stays
  • Position both properties within a competitive luxury market
  • Support seasonal promotions and occupancy goals

Strategy

  • Luxury hospitality storytelling
  • Wedding and event marketing
  • Organic reach campaigns for venue showcases
  • Destination-focused content creation
  • Cross-property brand positioning
  • Community engagement and guest communication

Execution

  • Developed cross-property brand positioning and a shared content strategy
  • Produced destination-focused photo and video content
  • Ran organic campaigns showcasing event and wedding spaces
  • Managed guest engagement and inquiries directly through social
